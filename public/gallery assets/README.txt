=============================================================
MUTHAMIZH ACADEMY - DYNAMIC GALLERY ASSETS
=============================================================

This directory is monitored automatically by the Muthamizh Academy Gallery:

1. Photos:
   Paste your photos (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif, .avif) into:
   public/gallery assets/Photos/

2. Videos:
   Paste your videos (.mp4, .webm, .mov, .m4v, .mkv) into:
   public/gallery assets/Videos/

HOW IT WORKS:
- No manual coding or configuration needed.
- As soon as you paste any image or video into these folders, it is automatically
  detected and displayed on the website at the Gallery tab!
- Titles are automatically formatted from the filenames (e.g. "broadcasting_floor.mp4" -> "Broadcasting Floor").
- Photos open in high-res lightbox modal with zoom and details.
- Videos feature built-in cinema player with playback, volume, and slow-motion controls.
